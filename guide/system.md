# Android Auto, the widget and the notification
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

## The notification and the lock screen

Music keeps playing with the app in the background or the phone locked. The media notification,
the lock screen and headphone buttons show the song and cover, with play, pause, next,
previous and the position bar. After a phone call, the music comes back where it was.

## Where the sound plays

**Output** in the player's tools row opens Android's output switcher: the phone's speaker,
Bluetooth headphones or another device the phone can play to. You can also pick it from the
media controls in the quick settings.

## Android Auto

In the car, own.music shows **Recently Played**, **Recently Added**, **Downloads**,
**Playlists**, **Artists** and **Favorites**, and you can search. Albums and playlists play from
the first song. If no server is added, it says **Add a server on your phone**.

## Widget

The **Recently Played** widget shows where you left off ("Continue · …") and the albums and
playlists you played last: one on the small widget, three on the medium one. Tap one to open the
app and play it. Add it the usual way: touch and hold the home screen, choose **Widgets**, then
own.music.

## Shortcut

Touch and hold the own.music icon for **Continue Listening**, which goes on with what was
playing.

## Google Assistant

You can ask the Assistant to play from own.music, for example "Play Kind of Blue on own.music",
"Play my playlist Road Trip on own.music" or "Continue listening on own.music". Albums and
playlists are looked up on the selected server. If nothing matches, the app says so.

## Background updates

About once an hour, the app updates in the background: it brings the copy of the library on the
phone up to date, sends plays and changes that were waiting, and refreshes the widget. Android
decides when it runs.
