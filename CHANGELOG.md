# Changelog

All notable changes to own.audio Music for Android. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

- F8 and M12: Android Auto (Recently Played, Recently Added, Downloads, Playlists, Artists, Favorites, search), the Recently Played widget, ownmusic:// links (resume, an album, a playlist, the player), the assistant ("play ‹album›", "play my playlist ‹name›", "continue listening") and a Continue Listening shortcut; the hourly refresh also refreshes the widget.
- Starting a narration can carry an Idempotency-Key (with a multipart boundary made from it), so a retried start never begins a second paid job.
- M10 and M11: on own.audio, your own songs, albums and artists get Choose Cover / Choose Picture, Share with the Family and Identify; concerts show their songs in the player, and the owner can edit them (names and start times); a concert resumes where you left it. Identify Library: runs over the whole library or a folder, the review by album with sure matches, other editions and other recordings, undo and delete.
- Navidrome and other Subsonic/OpenSubsonic servers, and Jellyfin (with Quick Connect and a Music Library choice), can be added and played from; their libraries are kept on the phone and read again every six hours on Wi-Fi or with Update Now. Plays reach each server its own way (now playing, progress, stopped; Last.fm's rule). Instant Mix on Jellyfin. The Playlists tab with playlists, smart playlists and the ready-made ones, the playlist page with its edits (rename, cover, share, delete, remove, reorder on own.audio), Add to Playlist, and the smart playlist rule editor with a live count. A favorite or rating the server refuses is undone on screen.
- F5, F6 and M6: downloads and offline — albums, playlists and songs downloaded in the background (two at a time, resumed after the app is killed, Wi-Fi only if chosen, Keep at Most with the least recently played going first), the Downloads tab, Offline Mode and the offline banner, browsing downloads or the whole library offline, favorites, ratings and plays made offline sent later in one batch, live updates while the app is open, and an hourly refresh in the background.
- The library side of smart playlists, playlist editing (description, reorder), sharing songs, albums and playlists with the family, your own covers and artist pictures (through uploads, by id), concert song marks and MusicBrainz identify (runs, review, live progress), all on own.audio's v2 API.
- M7 and M8 (library side): Subsonic/OpenSubsonic and Jellyfin as music sources — browsing, every album order the server has, favorites, ratings (Subsonic), playlists, lyrics, Instant Mix (Jellyfin), ReplayGain and normalization gain, smaller streams, plays and session reports, and the catalogue on the phone read page by page (the catalogue database moves to version 2 to keep format and gain).
- Players wait through long signal gaps instead of failing as "stuck buffering" after ten minutes.
- M4: the playback service and the music screens — Home (Shuffle All, Recently Played, Recently Added, Playlists), Library (artists, albums with every sort, songs, genres, favorites, concerts), album and artist pages, the song menu (Play Next, Add to Queue, Favorite, Rate), Search, the mini player and the full player with Queue, Sleep Timer, Crossfade and Volume Leveling, Lyrics and Equalizer; Settings gains Streaming and Playback. Plays in the background with the media notification, and the last queue comes back paused after a relaunch.
- A privacy policy (PRIVACY.md), linked from Settings → About.
- The music engine on Media3: playback links asked for when a song opens and renewed when they expire (never in a loop), the storage-route retry, a listening cache keyed by song rather than by link, a gapless queue whose edits never touch the playing song, crossfade on two players, ReplayGain from the server or the file's own tags (boosts through the equalizer's input gain), the 6-band equalizer, audio focus only from the first play, the decoder let go 30 s after a pause, and the last queue kept for the next launch.
- F7: the app shell and the own.audio screens — welcome, own.audio account (sign up, sign in, two-factor, Google in the Play build), Try the Demo Library, Add Server, Settings (servers, accent color, language, about, licences, logs), the server page (status and Check Now, sign in again, delete account, name, connection, addresses), Family with invitations and QR codes, Devices and Listening. Settings rows now use the text colour on their cards.
- M1 and M2: the music library layer — one model over all servers, the catalogue on the phone in Room, and own.audio as a source fed by the sync snapshot and change feed (a failed re-sync leaves the old catalogue usable; a cut snapshot resumes where it stopped).
- The player's logic, ported from the iOS app with its tests: the play queue (shuffle, repeat, Play Next, editing), ReplayGain, the equalizer presets, concert chapters, the play-counting rule, the sleep timer, the storage-route fallback and the audio payload check (which also recognises Matroska/WebM correctly — the iOS check never could).
- F3: `:core:servers` — server records in one encrypted file (the key stays in the Android Keystore), never writing stale tokens back; address scopes, racing by server identity and per-network memory; the network monitor (metered, Data Saver); the offline switch; saved answers for offline use.
- F2: `:kit:ownaudio`, the own.audio /api/v2 client: discovery (needs server 1.0.4), sign-in with password, two-factor and Google, rotating tokens refreshed one at a time, the catalogue, lists, smart playlists, state, playback links, sync snapshot and changes, live events, uploads, household, identify, narration and summaries. No /api/v1 calls.
- F1: the look of the iOS app — tokens, theme, accent choice, the animated own.audio mark, the rotating wordmark, the hero, shared components (cover art, mini player, offline banner, settings rows, download buttons, badges, QR code, Google button, accent picker), a debug design catalogue with screenshot tests, and the launcher icon from the iOS icon's construction.
- `:kit:subsonic` and `:kit:jellyfin`: the Subsonic/OpenSubsonic and Jellyfin clients, ported from the iOS app, with Quick Connect; Ampache is recognised and reported as not accepting token sign-in.
- F0: the build (Gradle 9.9.2, AGP 9.5.2, Kotlin 2.4.21, compileSdk 37), `play` and `foss` flavours, the module layout, five empty tabs in English and Czech, CI, the APK size check and the drift check against the sibling apps.
- Implementation plan and the shared Android foundation document.
